DESCRIPTIVE AVERAGEInsufficient comparable polls

The checked snapshot does not supply three compatible polling organizations within the declared 28-day window. We show the observations instead of inventing a trend.

How an average would be calculated

Same race, population and response convention; latest eligible observation per interviewing organization; a 28-calendar-day field-end window; at least three organizations; equal organization weights. Shared polling error remains. No confidence band or win probability is inferred.
